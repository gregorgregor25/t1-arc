# Sharing the T1 Arc demo

Link to the [film on the website](https://t1arc.com/#demo). GitHub’s README
links to that player; it does not depend on a GitHub video attachment.

Use the publication title **T1 Arc demo | AI narration: elevenlabs.io**.
Retain captions, privacy context and the treatment boundary. The film includes
the creator’s records with permission and an illustrative unsaved food draft.

The software’s MIT licence does not grant reuse of the film’s soundtrack or
narration. Read [credits, subtitles and media terms](../../docs/media/README.md)
before reusing it. Private raw captures and production journals are not distributed.
