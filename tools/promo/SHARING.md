## Accurate description and text alternative

T1 Arc brings glucose, insulin, food and health records into one Android app.
The film opens with Today and Health, then introduces Tarv1s. A genuine question
asks about yesterday's average glucose; the recorded answer follows an editorial
cut and a reading hold. Timeline places records in their daily context.

The refreshed food scene shows a real search and example portion in the current
app. It is an unsaved draft, not proof that a meal was logged. Some earlier scenes
contain the creator's own records with permission; the food capture uses a
synthetic emulator profile. Do not label the entire film as synthetic data.

The closing states that T1 Arc is open source, health history is stored on the
phone, and maintainers do not collect that history. Its qualifier says optional
AI sends relevant data directly to OpenAI. This does not mean every integration
is offline; see [Privacy](../../PRIVACY.md).

This edited demonstration is not a performance benchmark, treatment recommendation,
improved-health-outcome claim or proof that every integration and device has
passed acceptance testing. Preserve those distinctions in post copy. Earlier
V1–V3 sharing notes and archived reports are historical; their silent-film,
watch-scene and example-only descriptions are not current.
