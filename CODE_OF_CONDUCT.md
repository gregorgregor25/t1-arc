# Code of conduct

T1 Arc contributors must make participation safe, respectful and useful.

We welcome people regardless of identity, background, health condition,
technical experience or geography. Be precise without being dismissive. Assume
good intent, accept correction, distinguish evidence from opinion and focus
critique on the work.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, doxxing, publishing private communications, pressuring anyone to
share health information or credentials, and knowingly overstating clinical or
regional validation.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, commits or contributions that
breach this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
or GitHub reporting tools. Do not include another person's health information
in a conduct report unless strictly necessary and explicitly authorized.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters.
